# Responsible use

Enigma is intended for development, QA, accessibility testing, demonstrations, and
other controlled workflows on devices and services you are authorized to test.

Do not use Enigma to evade access controls, misrepresent activity, violate another
service's rules, stalk or deceive people, or interfere with safety-critical systems.
Location simulation can affect emergency, navigation, tracking, and account-security
features. Restore the device's real location after every test and verify it before
relying on location-aware software.

Enigma does not bypass Apple Trust, Developer Mode, or pairing approval and provides
no guarantee against account enforcement or third-party policy violations.
